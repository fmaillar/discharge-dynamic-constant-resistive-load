Overlap audit against JSEL-D-26-00904
-------------------------------------
The earlier manuscript addresses detrended fast voltage fluctuations, spectral
aliasing, harmonic phase locking, harmonic reconstruction, and a low-SOC
transition. The present manuscript addresses the slow discharge trajectory,
dV/dt as a function of terminal voltage, global temporal-rate scaling, and
voltage-dependent local departure.

Shared material is restricted to the underlying archived D70--D79 experiment,
basic acquisition facts, and the derived-current status. Those shared facts are
necessary to describe the reused experimental archive. The signal-processing
object, mathematical observables, figures, Results, Discussion, and central
claims are different.

The present manuscript explicitly states that it does not revisit the previous
spectral/harmonic claims. No spectral PSD, alias map, phase-locking metric,
harmonic reconstruction, low-SOC changepoint, or periodic-component variance
result is reused as a result of this paper.
